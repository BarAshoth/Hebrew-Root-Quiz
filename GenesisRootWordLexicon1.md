
## genesis 1 
📜 Genesis 1:1–1:5 Master Root Lexicon

Verse 1: "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

- **רֹּאשׁ (R-SH)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ בְּרֵאשִׁית (_B'reishit_ / "In the beginning")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **ROHSH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **Head, chief, summit, or firstfruits.** It refers to the physical head of a body or the primary, most vital choice part of a harvest. It implies a foundation of supreme importance, not just a starting position on a timeline.
- **בָּרָא (B-R-A)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ בָּרָא (_Bara_ / "Created")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **bah-RAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To shape, cut, carve out, or clear forest.** In the ancient world, it carried the physical imagery of a builder clearing away thick brush to create a defined space, or a sculptor carving an unformed block into a precise shape.
- **אֱלֹהַּ (E-L-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ אֱלֹהִים (_Elohim_ / "God")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **eh-LOH-ah**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **Power, might, strength, or a prominent pillar.** It derives from an older root meaning "to be strong" or "to bind together." It represents the ultimate, supreme force that anchors and holds everything in place.
- **שָׁמָה (SH-M-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ הַשָּׁמַיִם (_Ha-shamayim_ / "The heavens")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **shah-MAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **Loftiness, height, or the upper atmosphere.** It carries the concept of the visible sky and the unreachable expanse above the clouds.
- **אֶרֶץ (A-R-TS)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ הָאָרֶץ (_Ha-aretz_ / "The earth")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **EH-rets**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To break, crush, or step down on.** The root word relates to the firm, physical ground that you stamp with your feet—the low, solid floor beneath your body.

Verse 2: "The earth was without form, and void; and darkness was on the face of the deep..."

- **הָיָה (H-Y-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ הָיְתָה (_Haytah_ / "Was")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **hah-YAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To breathe, exist, fall out, or become.** It is an active word of breathing or coming into being, rather than a passive description of state.
- **תָּהָה (T-H-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ תֹהוּ (_Tohu_ / "Without form")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **tah-HAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To lie waste, be empty, or wilderness.** It represents a trackless, chaotic wasteland where there are no landmarks, roads, or boundaries—pure formlessness.
- **בָּהַה (B-H-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ בֹהוּ (_Bohu_ / "Void")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **bah-HAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To be empty, vacant, or completely hollow.** It implies an absolute vacuum containing zero substance.
- **חָשַׁךְ (CH-SH-K)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ חֹשֶׁךְ (_Choshek_ / "Darkness")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **chah-SHAK** (_the "ch" is a raspy sound from the throat_)
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To obscure, withhold, or dim.** It refers to the physical state of light being actively blocked, withdrawn, or hidden away.
- **פָּנָה (P-N-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ פְּנֵי (_P'nei_ / "Face of")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **pah-NAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To turn, look, or face toward.** It physically means the front part of an object or person—the side that is turned toward you to be seen.
- **הוּם (H-U-M)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ תְּהוֹם (_Tehom_ / "The deep")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **HOOM**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To roar, murmur, throw into confusion, or crash.** This root refers to a massive, raging, chaotic body of water like a roaring abyss or a stormy ocean shelf.
- **רוּחַ (R-U-CH)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ רוּחַ (_Ruach_ / "The Spirit")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **ROO-ach** (_the "ch" is a raspy sound from the throat_)
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **Wind, breath, exhalation, or violent scent.** It is the physical force of moving air or a life-giving breath that cannot be seen but can be felt.
- **רָחַף (R-CH-P)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ מְרַחֶפֶת (_Merachefet_ / "Moved/Hovered")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **rah-CHAF** (_the "ch" is a raspy sound from the throat_)
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To flutter, shake, brood, or grow soft.** It carries the imagery of a mother bird vibrating her wings over her eggs, keeping them warm and waiting for life to break out.
- **מָיִם (M-Y-M)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ הַמָּיִם (_Ha-mayim_ / "The waters")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **MAH-yim**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **Primitive liquid or moisture.** (Derived from a root meaning "watery substance").

    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **ah-MAHR**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To bring to light, utter, command, or boast.** It means to take a thought hidden inside the mind and speak it outward so it becomes a physical, audible reality.
- **אוֹר (A-U-R)**
    - _The Surface Words:_ אוֹר / יְהִי אוֹר (_Or_ / "Light")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **OHR**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To break dawn, shine, set on fire, or illuminate.** It is the elemental root for brightness, dawn, or ignition.

Verse 4: "And God saw the light, that it was good; and God divided the light from the darkness."

- **רָאָה (R-A-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ וַיַּרְא (_Vayar_ / "And He saw")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **rah-AH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To inspect, perceive, discern, or experience.** It means to closely examine the nature of an object, rather than just glance at it.
- **טוֹב (T-U-B)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ טוֹב (_Tov_ / "Good")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **TOHV**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **Functional, pleasant, complete, or fit for purpose.** In the ancient mind, "good" meant an item worked exactly the way it was engineered to work.
- **בָּדַל (B-D-L)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ וַיַּבְדֵּל (_Vayavdel_ / "And He divided")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **bah-DAHL**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To sever, separate, isolate, or set apart.** It means to draw a hard, physical boundary line between two things so they no longer touch or mix.

Verse 5: "God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night. So the evening and the morning were the first day."

- **קָרָא (K-R-A)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ וַיִּקְרָא (_Vayikra_ / "And He called")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **kah-RAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To encounter, cry out, summon, or assign a name.** It means to proclaim a title over something to establish your ownership or authority over it.
- **יוֹם (Y-U-M)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ יוֹם (_Yom_ / "Day")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **YOHM**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To be hot, heat, or the warm hours.** The root connects directly to the heat of the sun during its high arc in the sky.
- **לָיִל (L-Y-L)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ לַיְלָה (_Laylah_ / "Night")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **LAH-yil**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To fold back, twist, or wrap away.** It carries the physical concept of the light being folded up or wrapped in a dark shroud, hiding the landscape.
- **עָרַב (A-R-B)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ עֶרֶב (_Erev_ / "Evening")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **ah-RAHB**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To mix, blend, grow dusky, or become chaotic.** It is the time of day when colors blur together, shadows mix, and clear definitions disappear into gray darkness.
- **בָּקַר (B-K-R)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ בֹקֶר (_Boker_ / "Morning")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **bah-KAHR**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To plow open, split, break through, or seek out.** It physically means to cut open a furrow in the dirt. Morning is the moment when the first ray of sun literally cuts or plows through the dark horizon.
- **אֶחָד (E-CH-D)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ אֶחָד (_Echad_ / "One/First")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **eh-CHAD** (_the "ch" is a raspy sound from the throat_)
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To unify, tie together, or lock into one.** It refers to absolute unity or something bound tight as a single, indivisible block.

    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To manufacture, construct, produce, or press into service.** Unlike _Bara_(carving a new form from scratch), _Asah_ emphasizes the active labor of building, fashioning, or organizing pre-existing material into a completed product.
- **תַּחַת (T-CH-T)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ מִתַּחַת (_Mitachat_ / "Under")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **TAH-chat** (_the "ch" is a raspy sound from the throat_)
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **The underside, bottom floor, or depressed place.** It physically refers to the base area directly underneath a structure or ceiling.
- **עַל (A-L)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ מֵעַל (_Me'al_ / "Above")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **AHL**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To ascend, go up, or the top ridge.** It derives from a root meaning to scale heights, referencing the elevated platform or highest boundary layer.

Verse 9: "Then God said, 'Let the waters under the heavens be gathered together into one place, and let the dry land appear'; and it was so."

- **קָוָה (K-V-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ יִקָּווּ (_Yikavu_ / "Let them be gathered together")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **kah-VAH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To bind, twist together, collect, or wait expectantly.** It carries the vivid physical imagery of twisting raw fibers tightly together to form a strong cord. In reference to water, it means binding independent drops into a single, massive collective pool.
- **קוּם (M-K-M)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ מָקוֹם (_Makom_ / "Place")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **mah-KOHM**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To stand up, rise, or fix into a position.** Derived from the root _Koom_ ("to stand"). A "place" is not just abstract space; it is a fixed, established station where things are set upright and anchored.
- **יָבֵשׁ (Y-B-SH)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ הַיַּבָּשָׁה (_Ha-yabashah_ / "The dry land")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **yah-BESH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To wither, dry up, or drain of moisture.** It means to evaporate or bake liquid away until only a hard, parched surface remains.
- **רָאָה (R-A-H)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ וְתֵרָאֶה (_V'teira'eh_ / "And let it appear/be seen")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **rah-AH**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **To be inspected, unveiled, visible, or exposed to view.** (This is the passive form of the root found in verse 4, meaning the land is uncovered so it can be visually verified).
- **כֵּן (K-N)**
    - _The Surface Word:_ כֵּן (_Ken_ / "So/Thus")
    - _Phonetic Pronunciation:_ **KEN**
    - _The Raw Root Meaning:_ **Erect, straight, solid, or fixed.** When the text says "and it was so," the root implies the command became a permanent, firm, unyielding reality on the ground.
